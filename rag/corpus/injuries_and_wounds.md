# Injuries, Wounds, and Burns

## Minor cuts and wounds
Clean the wound with clean water and mild soap, remove visible dirt, and cover with a clean
dressing. Watch for signs of infection (increasing redness, swelling, warmth, pus, or fever) over
the following days.

## Severe bleeding wounds
Apply firm, direct pressure with a clean cloth and keep the injured part elevated if possible. Do
not remove an embedded object — stabilize it in place and refer immediately. Severe or
uncontrolled bleeding is always an emergency.

## Burns
Cool a burn immediately with clean, cool (not ice-cold) running water for at least 10-20 minutes.
Do not apply toothpaste, oil, or other home remedies. Cover loosely with a clean, non-stick
dressing. Refer to a facility if the burn is large, deep, on the face/hands/genitals, or shows
signs of infection.

## Suspected fractures
Immobilize the injured limb in the position found using a splint or firm support, and avoid
moving the patient unnecessarily. Refer to a facility with X-ray/orthopedic capability, especially
if there is visible deformity, inability to bear weight, or the bone is exposed.

## Head injury
Any head injury with loss of consciousness (even brief), repeated vomiting, confusion, unequal
pupil size, or a fit needs immediate referral for emergency assessment.
