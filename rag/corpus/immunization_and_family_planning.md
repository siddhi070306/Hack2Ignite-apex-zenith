# Immunization and Family Planning

## Routine childhood immunization
Encourage caregivers to complete the full immunization schedule on time (BCG, OPV, DPT/pentavalent,
measles-rubella, and boosters as per the national schedule). Mild fever or soreness at the
injection site after vaccination is normal and can be managed with rest and fluids; a very high
fever, persistent crying, swelling, or a fit after vaccination should be reported and assessed.

## Missed doses
A missed or delayed vaccine dose does not mean starting the schedule over — advise catching up on
missed doses at the next available session rather than skipping them.

## Family planning counseling
Provide accurate information on available family planning methods and refer to a health worker or
facility for method selection based on the individual's health and preferences. Respect the
person's choice and avoid pressuring any particular method.

## Adolescent health
Adolescents may be hesitant to discuss health concerns openly. Create a private, non-judgmental
space for conversation, and refer concerns about growth, nutrition, menstrual health, or mental
health to the appropriate health worker.
