# Vomiting and Diarrhea

## Assessing severity
Frequent vomiting or watery diarrhea (several episodes within a few hours), especially combined
with dehydration signs (sunken eyes, poor skin turgor, lethargy) or blood in the stool, needs
prompt referral to a Sub-Centre or CHC.

## Home management (no danger signs)
Continue ORS and normal feeding/breastfeeding. Avoid stopping food intake — small, frequent meals
are better tolerated than large ones. Monitor the frequency of loose stools/vomiting and watch
for dehydration signs over the next 12-24 hours.

## Referral criteria
Refer if: the patient cannot keep any fluids down, there is blood in vomit or stool, symptoms
persist beyond 24-48 hours without improvement, or any dehydration or other danger sign appears.
