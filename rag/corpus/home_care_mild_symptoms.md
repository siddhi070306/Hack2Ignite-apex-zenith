# Home Care for Mild Symptoms

## Cold and mild sore throat
Warm saline gargles (a pinch of salt in warm water), steam inhalation, and plenty of warm fluids
help relieve mild cold and sore throat symptoms. These are self-limiting and generally resolve
within a few days without needing referral.

## Mild body ache or tiredness
Rest, hydration, and monitoring are usually sufficient for mild, generalized body ache or
tiredness without fever or other symptoms. Encourage the patient to report if new symptoms
develop or existing ones worsen.

## When mild symptoms need reassessment
If a "mild" case develops fever above 102°F, breathing difficulty, persistent vomiting, or any
general danger sign, reassess and refer according to the relevant protocol rather than continuing
home care alone.
