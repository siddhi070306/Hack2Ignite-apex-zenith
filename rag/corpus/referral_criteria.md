# Referral Criteria by Facility Level

## When to refer to a District Hospital / CHC immediately (Red)
Any general danger sign (unconsciousness, convulsions, inability to drink/feed, severe bleeding,
cyanosis, severe chest indrawing), suspected cardiac emergency, or severe respiratory distress
warrants immediate referral to a facility with emergency and, where possible, ambulance capability.

## When a Sub-Centre / ANM visit within 24 hours is appropriate (Yellow)
Persistent fever without danger signs, moderate dehydration responding to ORS, mild-to-moderate
breathlessness without red-flag features, or symptoms that are not improving after a day of home
care are appropriately managed with a Sub-Centre or ANM assessment rather than an emergency
referral.

## When home care and monitoring is sufficient (Green)
Mild, self-limiting symptoms — a mild cold, sore throat, or low-grade fever without any danger
signs — can usually be managed at home with rest, fluids, and monitoring, with a follow-up if
symptoms worsen or do not resolve within a few days.
